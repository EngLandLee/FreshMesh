# Code of Conduct

Copyright 2026 FreshMesh-DXOS contributors. This project follows the Contributor
Covenant v2.1 principles.

## Our standards

We welcome respectful, precise collaboration across roles and backgrounds. Expected
behavior includes empathy, constructive review, acknowledgement of uncertainty, and
focus on the work. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination,
personal attacks, doxxing, threats, sexualized language or attention, and publishing
private information without consent.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately through the security contact in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)
or to the repository maintainers. Include context, dates, links, and witnesses where
safe to do so. Maintainers may investigate confidentially, ask for additional
information, remove harmful content, or temporarily/permanently restrict participation.
They will protect reporter privacy as far as practical and explain an outcome when
permitted. Retaliation against a good-faith report is not acceptable.
